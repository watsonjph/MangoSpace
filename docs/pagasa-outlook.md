# November 2026 PAGASA outlook

Source: DOST-PAGASA, El Niño Advisory No. 6, issued 3 November 2026.

- [Official advisory listing](https://www.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/climate/el-nino-la-nina/advisories)
- [Advisory and November rainfall map](https://pubfiles.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/pagasaweb/files/climate/monthlyclimateassessment/11052026_MCAO_EN_Advisory_6.pdf)

The November outlook groups the rest of the country, including Cebu and Bohol, under below-normal to way-below-normal rainfall. Its near-normal exceptions are Davao del Norte, Davao City and Agusan del Norte. The app preserves that combined range rather than assigning a more precise category from the small map. The advisory also warns that tropical cyclones and other weather systems can still bring heavy rain.

Both province entries in `outlook.json` summarize this November outlook. They do not predict town rainfall or extend the rainfall outlook through May. Hubas makes no forecast of its own.
